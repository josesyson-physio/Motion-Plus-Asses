Capture setup

  • Plain, light wall with no clutter behind the patient.
  • Shorts and fitted top, barefoot, hair clear of ears and neck.
  • Phone or tablet upright at hip height, about 3 m away, whole body from head to feet in frame.
  • Stand relaxed, feet hip-width, arms by the side, eyes level.

Optional white dots make points easier to place accurately.

  • Front: ASIS, centre of patella, mid-ankle.
  • Back: C7, PSIS, mid-heel.
  • Side: ear tragus, acromion, greater trochanter, lateral knee joint line, lateral malleolus.
  • Spine curves (side photos): C7, most rounded point of the upper back, T12, deepest point of the low back, PSIS and ASIS. Then tap Add spine markers.

Detection runs on this device. Faces are pixelated on screen and photos never leave the device or appear in reports.

Gait from video

  • Clear walkway of 6 to 8 m, or a treadmill at the patient's comfortable speed.
  • Phone in landscape or portrait at hip height, steady on a tripod or a shelf, about 3 m away.
  • Side view: the patient walks across the frame at their own pace. Back view: the patient walks straight away from the camera.
  • Record 8 to 12 seconds with at least 3 full strides in view. Shorts and fitted clothing help.
  • Record in app is the most reliable way: the app films and tracks the walk itself. Phone camera and gallery videos work too; 1080p is best, as 4K or "High efficiency" clips may not open on every phone.

What is measured. Side view: cadence, step length and timing, stance phase, knee and hip motion, trunk lean. Back view: pelvic drop, dynamic knee valgus, trunk side lean and step width.

Video is processed on this device and is not saved. Only the measured points go into the session file.
