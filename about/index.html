Where it starts

An AI pod in every neuro rehab centre.

A pod is a small, step-free workspace with two to four seats, basic computers running AI models, assistive tools and a 3D printer. People walk in and work, like an early-2000s net café. Seats grow with demand.

Pod

A place to work

Computers, AI tools, a 3D printer and screen readers, voice typing and switches, so the tool fits the person.

Marketplace

A source of paid work

Projects are posted, matched, delivered and paid for. Every finished project joins the maker's portfolio.

Network

People who support each other

Pods across the city are connected, so a project posted at one centre can be done at another.

  1. PostSmall businesses and startups post a project.
  2. ChooseAn app, a website, a design or marketing.
  3. DiscussMakers talk changes through with the client.
  4. Learn and buildTrained on the job, with AI at their side.
  5. Get paidPayment on delivery. The work joins their portfolio.

Like a freelancing website, with a real place to work. Every finished project brings the next one in.

Already running

In child ability centres, we find a child's hidden strength early.

The Blossoming Mind talent assessment is running in Hyderabad. Physiotherapy knowledge, therapists' insight and structured scoring across 10 talent domains, each scored 1 to 5, are joined by AI into one strengths profile.

A real example

A score of 1 is a starting line. An 8-year-old needed support in all ten areas. Four kinds of knowledge looked again, and found joy, a sibling bond and a love of the outdoors.

PhysiotherapistSpecial educatorTherapistsAI

The result is a strengths profile, a 90-day plan, future tech support such as picture-choice communication and visual routines, and a path to financial stability. Unseen to Unstoppable.

By the physio

By physios. For everyone.

Physiotherapists read how a body moves, balances and grows. That is why they are often the first to notice what a person can do, not only what they cannot. Inclusive.Si grows out of that habit of looking again.

The idea was born inside MotionPlus Physio's rehabilitation and child ability centres, and every pod keeps clinical care and a person's strengths at the centre.
