The engine

Anyone can post a problem. Anyone can solve it and earn.

Pods are physical spaces with computers, AI tools and 3D printers, placed inside rehab and child ability centres. The first pilot is planned at Uniquorns and Smart Rehab in Hyderabad, then across India.

Post

Centres, small businesses and outsiders post a problem. People who cannot afford market rates can still get work done.

Build

A minimal space with 2 to 4 seats to begin, basic computers, AI models and a 3D printer. Seats grow over time.

Earn

Paid on delivery. Every finished project joins the maker's portfolio and brings the next problem in.

Who we include

Inclusion is bigger than disability.

Inclusive.Si is for people held back physically, financially, socially or by lack of resources. We begin with people with physical disabilities, because that is where the model is hardest to get right.

  • PhysicalAn accessible workplace inside a centre they already visit.
  • FinancialShared equipment and AI tools at no or low cost.
  • SocialA marketplace that brings work to them, and a public record of what they finished.
  • ResourcesLearning by doing real projects next to other makers.

Already running

From unseen to unstoppable.

The Blossoming Mind talent assessment, built with our physiotherapists, looks at 10 domains scored 1 to 5 and turns them into a strengths profile called My Spark. It is running with children in Hyderabad.

01UnseenA low score is a starting line, not a verdict.
02SeenPhysiotherapists and therapists look again and find what stands out.
03UnstoppableA strengths profile, a care pathway and a route to independent income through skills.
